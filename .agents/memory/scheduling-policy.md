Retain the tutor's travel buffer when an in-person lesson is followed by an online lesson. Do not assume the tutor can start teaching online immediately from the previous location.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose to keep travel time before online lessons when asked about an in-person lesson ending immediately beforehand.

**How to apply:** Distinguish lesson mode from a tutor's overall ability to offer both modes. Preserve travel protection for in-person transitions; consecutive online lessons do not inherently require travel.

Offer valid quarter-hour starts for longer lessons too, rather than restricting 60- or 90-minute lessons to half-hour starts.

**Why:** The user's example is a 4:00–6:15 pm availability window that should fit two one-hour in-person lessons at 4:00 and 5:15 with a 15-minute travel gap.

**How to apply:** Keep the times shown to parents consistent with server-side availability and conflict checks. Preferred start suggestions must not be confused with whether another time is actually bookable.
