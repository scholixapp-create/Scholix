---
name: Student lesson addresses
description: Privacy and migration policy for addresses used at in-person lessons.
---

Store in-person lesson addresses on each student profile, not by reusing the parent's profile address. Require the parent to provide each student's address, and expose it only to the assigned tutor for an active in-person booking.

**Why:** A parent's profile address may not be the student's lesson location, and copying it silently can disclose or route a tutor to the wrong place.

**How to apply:** Keep parent address fields private and separate; prompt for missing student addresses before in-person bookings and do not include them in tutor profile listings, invoices, or historical session output.
