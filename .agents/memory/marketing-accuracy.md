---
name: Marketing accuracy
description: Scholix's rule for public statistics and social-proof copy.
---

Use verified real numbers for public claims. If real numbers are not impressive, replace the numbers with factual product statements. Do not invent customer testimonials, ratings, tutor counts, session totals or earnings.

**Why:** The user flagged fake numbers on the homepage and About page and specifically asked for statements instead when real numbers are not impressive.

**How to apply:** Apply this to future public marketing copy and design changes. Keep actual pricing and commission policies distinct from claims about customer activity or results.
